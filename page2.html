How to Add Oranges to Your Diet

Simple Steps to Enjoy Oranges

  1. Peel the orange and eat it fresh as a morning snack.
  2. Squeeze them to make fresh, natural orange juice.
  3. Cut them into slices and toss them into a healthy salad.

Adding oranges to your daily routine is incredibly simple. Because they have a natural protective peel, they are perfect for taking on the go without needing plastic wrap.

Whether you drink them, eat them whole, or mix them into meals, oranges are a versatile fruit that can improve your overall diet.
